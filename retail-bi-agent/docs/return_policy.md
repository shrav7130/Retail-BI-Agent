# UrbanCart Retail — Return and Refund Policy

Version 2.4 | Effective 1 July 2025 | Owner: Customer Experience

## 1. General rule
Customers may return most items within 30 days of purchase with the original receipt or order number. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## 2. Category rules

### Electronics
- Returns accepted within 10 days if the item is unopened or defective.
- Opened electronics that are not defective cannot be returned.
- Defective electronics are replaced free of charge within the 1-year manufacturer warranty.

### Grocery
- Grocery items are not returnable.
- Exception: damaged, spoiled or expired items reported within 48 hours get a full refund or replacement.

### Apparel
- Returns accepted within 30 days if tags are attached and the item is unworn and unwashed.
- Socks and innerwear are not returnable for hygiene reasons.

### Home
- Returns accepted within 30 days if unused and in original packaging.
- Bedsheets and towels cannot be returned once the packaging is opened.

### Beauty
- Only unopened and sealed products can be returned within 15 days.
- Opened beauty products can be returned only if the customer reports an allergic reaction, with a photo of the product.

## 3. Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
- Cash purchases are refunded as cash up to Rs 5,000. Above that, a bank transfer is used.
- Customers may choose store credit instead, valid for 12 months.

## 4. Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size, colour or variant are free within 30 days (10 days for Electronics), subject to stock.

## 5. Returns across stores
An item bought at one UrbanCart store can be returned at any other UrbanCart store.
