# UrbanCart Retail — Promotions Calendar (October 2025 – December 2026)

Maintained by Marketing. Last updated 1 October 2026.

## Festive season 2025
- 1 October 2025 to 31 December 2025: festive campaign across all stores. Store traffic is normally highest from October to November.

## Electronics Clearance Promotion
- 1 January 2026 to 31 August 2026: 15% off all Electronics, automatically applied. See the Discount Policy, section 4.1.
- Marketing ran in-store posters and email campaigns for Electronics throughout this period.
- The promotion ended on 31 August 2026 and was not extended.

## September 2026
- No company-wide promotion was scheduled in September 2026.
- Marketing activity returned to normal, non-promotional levels.
- Pune Camp renovation ran from 8 to 17 September (see the Store Operations Guide, section 4.1).

## Festive season 2026
- 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026: festive campaign planned across all stores, with offers on Grocery, Apparel, Home and Beauty.
- Electronics is not part of the festive offers unless Head Office announces otherwise.

## Rules for planning promotions
- Promotions need Head Office approval at least 14 days before launch.
- Every promotion must have a stated start and end date.
- Marketing and Finance review results within 30 days after each promotion ends.
