## 2. Opening hours
All stores are open 10:00 to 21:00, seven days a week. Weekends are the busiest days, with roughly 30% more customers than weekdays.

## 3. Stock and replenishment
- Replenishment trucks arrive on Tuesdays and Fridays.
- Store managers must report stock-outs of top-selling products within 24 hours.

## 4. Store renovations and temporary changes
- Renovations are scheduled by Retail Operations and need Head Office approval.
- During a renovation, the store stays open where it is safe to do so, but only part of the floor and billing counters are available.
- Reduced footfall is expected during renovation periods, and sales targets for that period are not enforced.

### 4.1 Pune Camp renovation (September 2026)
- Dates: 8 September to 17 September 2026 (10 days).
- Scope: new flooring, lighting and billing counters.
- During the works, only the ground-floor entrance was open and only 2 of the 6 billing counters worked.
- Retail Operations expected footfall to drop by around 80% in this period.
- The store returned to full operation on 18 September 2026.

## 5. Monthly store review
Each month, Regional Managers review store sales against the previous month and the same month last year. Stores with temporary closures or renovations are reviewed separately and not ranked against fully open stores.

## 6. Customer data
Store staff must not share customer purchase history with anyone outside UrbanCart. Reports to Head Office use only aggregated figures.
