# UrbanCart Retail — Discount Policy

Version 3.2 | Effective 1 October 2025 | Owner: Commercial Finance

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines who can give discounts, how discounts combine, and how category-wide promotions are approved and ended. It applies to all five UrbanCart stores.

## 2. Discount authority levels
- Cashiers may give up to 5% off at their discretion for damaged packaging or minor complaints.
- Store managers may approve up to 10% off a single bill.
- Regional managers may approve up to 20% off a single bill.
- Any discount above 20%, or any discount across a whole category, needs written Head Office approval.

## 3. Stacking rules
- Only one promotional discount can apply to an item at a time.
- Manager discounts cannot be added on top of an active category promotion.
- Loyalty points can be redeemed on top of any discount, but cannot exceed 20% of the bill value.

## 4. Category promotions

### 4.1 Electronics Clearance Promotion
- Dates: 1 January 2026 to 31 August 2026.
- Offer: 15% off every Electronics product, applied automatically at checkout in all stores.
- Purpose: clear previous-generation stock and grow the Electronics category.
- Status: ENDED on 31 August 2026. The promotion was not renewed.
- Reason for ending: Finance's margin review found that Electronics margins during the promotion fell below the company's 30% category margin floor, so the promotion could not continue.
- Renewal: any new Electronics promotion needs Head Office approval. None is scheduled for September 2026.

### 4.2 Festive Season Offers
- Dates: 1 October to 31 December each year.
- Offer: store-wide offers of up to 10% on Grocery, Apparel, Home and Beauty products. Electronics is excluded unless Head Office announces a separate promotion.

## 5. Price matching
UrbanCart does not match competitor prices. Store managers may use the discount authority in section 2 instead.

## 6. Ending a promotion
Promotions end automatically on their stated end date. Stores should expect demand for the affected category to move back to its normal level within the first month after a promotion ends.
